- Decision, 2026-10-19: the human owner selected the recommended label-loss policy for the action view: once the view loses its pinned sign-in label through an opener or a grant under a different label, the loss is final for that view instance. The selection stays cleared, every later opener (the old label, the new label or an unreadable result) is ignored, and Play, Pause and Resume cannot reactivate the view or issue work; recovery is a new view or a reload. This resolves only the label-loss choice of the design approved at [7836c80345cec2aeb474d2eb026a1b9455819ef2](https://github.com/MachineWisdomAI/saga-a2ui/blob/7836c80345cec2aeb474d2eb026a1b9455819ef2/blueprints/features/request_view_lifetimes.blueprint.md).
